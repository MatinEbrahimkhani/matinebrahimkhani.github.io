PerSpaCor: Correcting Space and ZWNJ Errors in Persian Text with Transformer Models

Matin Ebrahimkhani, Ebrahim Ansari

  • Novel Research: Engineered and evaluated four original transformer-based classifier architectures—ReLU-Norm, DualStep DropNet, and SimplexNet—achieving a State-of-the-Art macro F1-score of 97.26%.

In Progress

Efficient Adaptation of Foundation Models: A Comparative Study of Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning Methods for Domain-Specific Tasks

Matin Ebrahimkhani, Mina Sirinchi, Mo Nasher, Mobin Ebrahimkhani

Investigating parameter-efficient fine-tuning techniques (LoRA, QLoRA, Adapters, Prefix Tuning, and full fine-tuning) across multiple NLP tasks. The study evaluates accuracy/F1, BLEU/ROUGE, and resource metrics (training time/GPU memory, inference latency, and number of trained parameters) to compare performance–efficiency trade-offs. Planned code and scripts will be released for reproducibility.
